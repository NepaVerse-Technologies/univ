About the college

A campus built around one programme, run properly.

Univ College was established to offer a single, focused undergraduate programme rather than a wide prospectus of unrelated courses. Every classroom, faculty appointment, and library acquisition serves the T.U. Bachelor of Business Studies syllabus.

Our lecturers set and mark the internal assessments themselves, sit on T.U.'s subject committees, and hold office hours students actually use — the corridor outside the accounts faculty room is busier than the canteen before internals.

Admissions

How to join the BBS programme

  1. 1

    Submit the application form

    Online or at the campus admission counter, along with SLC/SEE and +2 transcripts.

  2. 2

    Sit the entrance assessment

    A short written test in English and general aptitude, held on campus.

  3. 3

    Interview & seat confirmation

    A brief interview, followed by fee submission to confirm your seat.

  4. 4

    T.U. registration

    The college handles your registration with Tribhuvan University directly.
